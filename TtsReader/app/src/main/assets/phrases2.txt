# 第二批：美国日常口语与职场地道用语（不与第一批重复，排除课本常见句）
# phrase | example sentence | 中文解释
my bad | Sorry, my bad. I sent you the wrong file. | 我的错，不好意思（口语道歉）
I'm down | Pizza tonight? Yeah, I'm down. | 我愿意，我参加
I'm good | Want another drink? No, I'm good, thanks. | 不用了，我这样就行（婉拒）
no worries | You're five minutes late? No worries. | 没事，别担心
it is what it is | The flight got cancelled. It is what it is. | 事已至此，就这样吧
hang tight | Hang tight, the doctor will be with you in a minute. | 稍等一下，别走开
sounds like a plan | Meet at seven by the fountain? Sounds like a plan. | 好主意，就这么定了
you bet | Can you cover my shift? You bet. | 当然可以，没问题
fair enough | I'd rather not work Sundays. Fair enough. | 有道理，可以理解
I feel you | Mondays are rough. I feel you. | 我懂你，深有同感
hit me up | If you're in town next week, hit me up. | 联系我，找我
catch you later | I've got to run. Catch you later. | 回头见
my two cents | Just my two cents, but I'd wait for the sale. | 我个人的一点看法
heads-up | Quick heads-up: the parking lot is closed tomorrow. | 提前打个招呼，预先提醒
bandwidth | I don't have the bandwidth to take on another client this month. | 精力、时间、余力
loop someone in | Can you loop me in on the vendor emails? | 把某人拉进来（让其知情或参与）
take it offline | This is getting detailed. Let's take it offline after the meeting. | 会后单独讨论
low-hanging fruit | Fix the typos first. That's low-hanging fruit. | 最容易做到、最容易见效的事
ping me | Ping me when the build is done. | 给我发个消息
action item | The only action item from today is to update the roadmap. | 待办事项，行动项
hard stop | I have a hard stop at three for another call. | 到点必须结束（不能延时）
deep dive | Next week we'll do a deep dive into the churn numbers. | 深入分析，细致研究
boil the ocean | We can't fix every bug at once. Let's not boil the ocean. | 想一口气把所有事都做完（不切实际）
ballpark figure | I don't need an exact quote, just a ballpark figure. | 大概的数字，粗略估计
back-of-the-envelope | A back-of-the-envelope calculation says we'd break even in a year. | 粗略估算的
buy-in | We need buy-in from the sales team before launching. | 认同和支持，同意配合
push back | The engineers pushed back on the two-week deadline. | 反对，提出异议
wear many hats | At a startup you wear many hats: sales, support, and IT. | 身兼数职
level set | Before we plan, let's level set on what the customer actually asked for. | 先统一认识、对齐基本情况
EOD | Please send the draft by EOD Friday. | 当天下班前（end of day）
OOO | I'll be OOO next week, so email Sarah instead. | 不在办公室，休假中（out of office）
on the same call | Were you on the same call? I don't remember agreeing to that. | 我们听到的是同一件事吗（表示疑惑）
put a pin in it | Good point. Let's put a pin in it and come back after lunch. | 先记下来，稍后再谈
raise a flag | I want to raise a flag about the server costs before we sign. | 提出警示，指出问题
per my last email | Per my last email, the invoice is already paid. | 如我上封邮件所说（常带不耐烦语气）
go live | The new website goes live on Monday. | 正式上线
open floor | That's all from me. I'll open the floor to questions. | 开放提问或讨论
one-pager | Can you write a one-pager on the proposal for the execs? | 一页纸的摘要
parking lot | Let's put the pricing question in the parking lot for now. | 暂存区（会议中把跑题的议题先放一边）
sync up | Let's sync up Thursday to compare notes. | 碰头对一下进度
offline | I'll answer that offline so we don't hold up the group. | 会后私下（处理）
bandwidth check | Quick bandwidth check: can anyone take the Friday demo? | 问问谁还有空、有余力
stretch goal | Ten thousand signups is our stretch goal. | 挑战性目标，理想目标
guardrails | Set some guardrails so the interns don't touch production. | 防护措施，边界规则
north star | Customer retention is our north star this year. | 核心目标，指导方向
throughput | Adding a second reviewer doubled our throughput. | 处理量，吞吐量
paper trail | Put the agreement in writing so there's a paper trail. | 书面记录，证据链
red tape | It took months of red tape to get the permit. | 繁文缛节，官僚手续
brownie points | He brought donuts to score brownie points with the boss. | 讨好赚来的印象分
carrot and stick | Management uses a carrot and stick approach: bonuses and warnings. | 奖惩并用，软硬兼施
bring to the table | What does the new hire bring to the table? | 能贡献什么，有什么价值
across the board | Prices went up ten percent across the board. | 全面地，无一例外
game plan | What's the game plan for the client visit? | 行动计划，策略
touch and go | It was touch and go for a while, but the patient pulled through. | 情况不稳定，悬而未决
crunch time | It's crunch time. The demo is in two days. | 关键冲刺期
ramp up | It takes new engineers about a month to ramp up. | 逐步上手；逐步增加
wind down | The project is winding down, so the team is shrinking. | 逐步收尾，减少
onboard | We onboard new customers with a thirty-minute call. | 让新人或新客户入门、上手
pivot | The app failed as a game, so the company pivoted to education. | 转向，调整方向
scope creep | The client keeps adding features. Classic scope creep. | 需求不断扩大，范围蔓延
band-aid fix | Restarting the server is a band-aid fix. We need the real cause. | 治标不治本的临时补救
nickel and dime | The hotel nickel and dimes you with fees for everything. | 处处收小钱，斤斤计较
cut corners | They cut corners on the wiring, and now it's a fire hazard. | 偷工减料，走捷径
on the clock | I can't chat right now. I'm on the clock. | 在上班时间，在干活
off the clock | Ask me about it after five, when I'm off the clock. | 下班了，不在工作时间
clock out | Don't forget to clock out before you leave. | 打卡下班
call in sick | I called in sick with a fever this morning. | 打电话请病假
pull one's weight | Everyone on this team pulls their weight. | 尽自己的本分，出力
slack off | Stop slacking off and finish the report. | 偷懒，懈怠
flake on someone | She flaked on me again. We were supposed to meet at eight. | 放某人鸽子，爽约
bail | The party was boring, so we bailed early. | 提前溜走，退出
crash | Can I crash on your couch tonight? | 借宿，随便睡一晚
grab a bite | Want to grab a bite before the movie? | 随便吃点东西
chip in | Let's all chip in ten dollars for the gift. | 凑钱，分摊
split the bill | Let's just split the bill three ways. | 平摊账单，AA
go Dutch | On the first date we went Dutch. | 各付各的
on me | Dinner is on me tonight. | 我请客
on the house | The dessert is on the house. Enjoy. | 店家免费赠送
tap water | Just tap water for me, please. | 自来水（餐馆里指免费水）
to-go | Can I get this to-go? | 打包带走
doggy bag | We couldn't finish, so we asked for a doggy bag. | 打包剩菜的袋子
pet peeve | Loud chewing is my biggest pet peeve. | 最受不了的小事，特别讨厌的事
bucket list | Seeing the northern lights is on my bucket list. | 人生愿望清单
guilty pleasure | Reality TV is my guilty pleasure. | 明知不好却很享受的小癖好
comfort food | Mac and cheese is the ultimate comfort food. | 治愈系食物，让人安心的食物
binge-watch | We binge-watched the whole season in one weekend. | 追剧，一口气看完
doomscrolling | I spent an hour doomscrolling the news last night. | 停不下来地刷负面新闻
adulting | Paying taxes and cooking dinner. Adulting is hard. | 像大人一样处理生活琐事
hangry | Feed him before he gets hangry. | 饿到发脾气
sketchy | That website looks sketchy. Don't enter your card number. | 可疑的，不靠谱的
legit | The deal is legit. I checked the reviews. | 真的，靠谱的
bummer | The concert got cancelled? What a bummer. | 真扫兴，真遗憾
flaky | He's flaky. Don't count on him showing up. | 不靠谱，常放鸽子
salty | He's still salty about losing the game. | 不服气，耿耿于怀
extra | She wore a ball gown to a picnic. So extra. | 太夸张，太用力
bougie | Twelve-dollar toast? That cafe is so bougie. | 装高档，小资气
no cap | This is the best burger in town, no cap. | 不骗你，说真的
sus | He left right before the money went missing. That's sus. | 可疑
it's giving | That outfit is giving nineties supermodel. | 有…的感觉，像是…（评价风格）
slay | You slayed that presentation. | 表现极佳，惊艳全场
rizz | He's got rizz. He talks to strangers like old friends. | 撩人的魅力，社交魅力
ick | The way he chews with his mouth open gives me the ick. | 突然的反感，膈应
main character energy | She walked in with main character energy. | 主角气场
touch grass | You've been arguing online all day. Go touch grass. | 别宅了，出去接触现实
glow up | She had a serious glow up after college. | 脱胎换骨的变美变强
say less | Free tickets tonight? Say less. | 不用多说，我懂了（欣然同意）
bet | Pick you up at eight? Bet. | 好的，成交
period | That's the best pizza in the city, period. | 就是这样，没得争
deadass | I'm deadass tired. I haven't slept in two days. | 真的，认真地（强调）
mood | Staying in bed all Sunday? Mood. | 太有共鸣了，就是我

low blow | Bringing up his divorce was a low blow. | 下作的攻击，卑劣的一击
